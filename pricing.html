Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, Huggy is commitment-free. You can cancel your Pro or Studio plan with a single click from your settings. You'll maintain access until the end of the current billing cycle.
What kind of apps can I build?
Almost anything that runs on the web: SaaS Dashboards, E-commerce, Portfolios, Blogs, API Wrappers, and more. Huggy follows React and TypeScript best practices.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption for your code and API keys. Your data remains your exclusive property.
