Questions before you choose

What is the difference between Credits and Cloud Balance?
Credits are for creating and improving apps with Huggy. Cloud Balance is for keeping published apps online: hosting, database, storage, bandwidth, realtime and deployed AI usage. You see simple usage numbers, not raw provider costs.
What happens if my app exceeds the included Cloud usage?
Free stays capped so there are no surprises. Pro can add Cloud top-ups when a live app grows. Scale supports auto top-up so teams can keep production apps running smoothly.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. You can cancel Pro or Scale from settings and keep access until the end of the billing cycle. Your projects remain in your workspace.
What kind of apps can I build?
Huggy is built for web products: SaaS dashboards, portals, booking tools, e-commerce, internal tools, AI utilities, portfolios and landing pages. New apps use modern React, TypeScript and Vite patterns by default.
Is my data secure?
Huggy keeps platform auth separate from generated app auth, protects secrets from the frontend, and treats your code and project data as your property. Sensitive keys should stay server-side only.
Will I lose my projects if I downgrade or cancel?
No. Projects stay attached to your account. Plan limits may affect publishing, top-ups or premium features, but Huggy should not remove your saved work unless you delete it.
Do published apps change when I keep editing?
No. The preview can evolve while the live app stays stable. The published version changes only after you click Publish again.
