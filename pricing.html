Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Credits and Cloud Balance?
Credits are used to build, edit and iterate with Huggy. Cloud Balance is used by published apps for hosting, database, storage, bandwidth, realtime and deployed AI usage. Users see credits and balance only, never internal provider costs.
What happens if my app exceeds the included Cloud usage?
Huggy keeps apps predictable: Free is capped, Pro can add Cloud top-ups, and Scale supports auto top-up. We notify you before heavy usage becomes a problem.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, Huggy is commitment-free. You can cancel your Pro or Scale plan from your settings and keep access until the end of the current billing cycle.
What kind of apps can I build?
Almost anything that runs on the web: SaaS Dashboards, E-commerce, Portfolios, Blogs, API Wrappers, and more. Huggy follows React and TypeScript best practices.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption for your code and API keys. Your data remains your exclusive property.
