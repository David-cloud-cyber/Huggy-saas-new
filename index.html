FAQ

Questions before you build.

Web products: SaaS dashboards, portals, booking tools, e-commerce, internal tools and landing pages. Huggy opens a builder workspace and turns your idea into a working preview, then a publishable app.

Real apps. For app requests, Huggy builds structured React, TypeScript and Vite projects — with files, styles, interactions and a live preview — not static mockups.

Credits power building and improving apps with AI. Paid plans include more credits and higher limits. Cloud, storage and deployed AI usage are tracked separately as your live apps grow.

Preview is where you inspect and iterate safely. Your live site changes only when you click Publish, so visitors never see unfinished work.

Provider keys and private tokens never appear in generated frontend code. Server-side secrets stay in secure backend environments, and sensitive actions stay confirmed and server-safe.
